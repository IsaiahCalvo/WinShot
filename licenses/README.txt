Licence texts that ship with WinShot.

MIT and Apache-2.0 attach their notice obligations to the distributed binary, so
these files are copied next to WinShot.exe by the build and carried by the
installer. The About pane links here.

  Apache-2.0.txt
      The full Apache License 2.0, required by ZXing.Net (which ships no licence
      file of its own — only the SPDX expression in its .nuspec).

  SkiaSharp-NativeAssets-THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt
      Verbatim from skiasharp.nativeassets.win32/3.119.4. SkiaSharp's own wrapper
      is MIT, but the native libSkiaSharp it ships embeds Google's Skia (BSD-3)
      and Skia's own dependencies. This is the authoritative list for those.

The per-package summary lives in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md beside WinShot.exe.
